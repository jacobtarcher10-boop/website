Our story

Her name. His name. One label.

Rose Archer began with a wardrobe complaint: everything worth wearing on the nights that matter was either built badly or priced absurdly. Olivia designs — a fashion student with a ruler in one hand and strong opinions about seam allowances in the other. Jacob runs everything else. We cut small, considered drops instead of endless collections, in fabrics we’d want against our own skin, sized UK 6 to 16 and fitted on real bodies.

When a piece sells out, we don’t remake it. We’d rather you loved something rare than liked something everywhere.

— Olivia & Jacob
